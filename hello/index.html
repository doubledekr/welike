Stop asking “what do you want to do?”

Let’s Together is a swipe app for two. You each say yes or no to movies, places to eat, things to do and trips. When you both say yes to the same thing, it’s a match, and the decision is made. Swipe whenever you like, with no pressure, and use what you’ve both liked to plan a night or decide in the moment. Let’s do more together.

The story

Why I built this

My partner and I kept having the same conversation. “What do you want to watch?” “I don’t know, what do you want to watch?” Forty minutes later we would still be scrolling, or we would give up and put on something neither of us picked.

The problem was never a lack of options. It was that neither of us wanted to be the one to choose, and neither of us knew what the other was quietly up for. So I built something that asks us both separately and only speaks up when we agree.

It started with movies. Then dinner, then things to do around town, then trips we might take one day. Now it can put a few of those together into a whole day out. It is still just me building it, usually at night, and it changes almost daily. You are one of the first people to use it, which is why your bugs and ideas on this page matter so much.

Dave

How it works

Swipe, match, decide

1
You both join with one couple codeOne of you starts a couple and texts the invite. The other opens the link and enters the code. Nothing can match until you are both in.
2
Swipe a few cards a dayMovies on your streaming services, restaurants near you, bars and museums, events this week, trips, even art. A small handful each day, so it takes two minutes.
3
Matches do the decidingYou only see what you both liked. Tap “Tonight” whenever you want to decide, and pick from your matches, or “Plan a day” to build an outing from them.
One important step

Add it to your Home Screen

This is what makes it run full screen like a normal app, and it is the only way it can send you a notification when your person picks something. In a browser tab it can’t reach you at all.

iPhoneSafariiOS 18 and earlier
  1. 1Open letstogether.app and tap the Share button at the bottom (a square with an arrow pointing up).
  2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3Tap Add, then open the app from the new icon.
iPhoneSafari on iOS 26the newest iPhone software
  1. 1Open letstogether.app and tap Share in the middle of the bar at the bottom. No Share button there? Tap ••• next to the address bar, then Share.
  2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3Tap Add at the top right, then open the app from the new icon.
iPhoneChromeneeds iOS 16.4 or later
  1. 1Open letstogether.app and tap the Share button at the top right, in the address bar.
  2. 2Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. 3Tap Add, then open the app from the new icon.

No “Add to Home Screen” in the list? Open the link in Safari and use the Safari steps.

AndroidChrome
  1. 1Open letstogether.app and tap the three-dot menu at the top right.
  2. 2Tap Add to Home screen (it may say Install app).
  3. 3Tap Install, then open the app from the new icon.

Then open it from the icon, not the browser. If it asks you to sign in, enter your couple code and the same name you used before, and say yes when it asks about notifications.

Why isn’t it in the App Store?

Because it is still being tested and I change it almost every day. A Home Screen app updates itself the moment I fix something, with nothing for you to download, and it works the same on iPhone and Android. Once it settles down, a store version may follow.

What it can do

Things worth trying

Plan a dayBuilds a day out from places you both liked, checks they are open, and sends it to your person to say yes or swap something. There is a night-in version too.
What should we do tonight?Deals you both the same cards right now. One match is the answer. Several go to a quick game, and the winner picks.
Go outBars, museums, bookstores, arcades and this week’s events near home, with a nudge before an event you liked passes.
Send one pickFound the one? Send it straight over with the trailer up front. They answer “let’s do it” or “try again”.
TripsSwipe on things to do in cities around the world. When a city fills up with matches, the app suggests a weekend there.
ArtSwipe on museum pieces. Matches go into a shared gallery and show which museum holds them.
Scratch cardEvery so often, a peek at one thing your person liked that you have not matched on yet.
Travel modeAway from home? It offers places to eat and things to do where you are, on your phone only.
